Good to know

Holiday lighting FAQs

Can you install lights I already purchased?

Yes, you can request an assessment for customer-provided lights. Please include the brand, model and photos of the proposed installation area. Compatibility and suitability depend on the specific project.

Do you offer installation and removal?

Installation and seasonal takedown can be discussed as part of your quote. Availability and scope are confirmed before scheduling.

How much does installation cost?

Pricing depends on roofline length, height, access, lighting type, materials and whether takedown is included. Send photos for a tailored estimate.

Do you offer Govee permanent lighting installation?

We can review your specific Govee product and installation request to determine whether it is within the services we can offer. Electrical work or other specialized work may require an appropriately licensed contractor.

When should I schedule?

Earlier booking provides the best chance of securing your preferred dates. Holiday-season availability can change quickly.
