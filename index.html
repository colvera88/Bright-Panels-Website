Solar Panel Cleaning

Professional cleaning designed for solar panel surfaces

Solar panels are exposed year-round to dust, pollen, bird droppings, dirt, and airborne debris. Over time, buildup can leave the surface visibly dirty and reduce the amount of sunlight reaching the panel.

Bright Panels uses purified water and professional soft-brush equipment to clean solar panel surfaces without harsh pressure washing or abrasive cleaning methods.

Our goal is simple: remove surface buildup carefully while treating the solar array and surrounding property with respect.

Solar Panel Bird Proofing

Help discourage birds from nesting beneath your solar panels

Solar panels can create a sheltered space where pigeons and other birds may attempt to nest. Over time, feathers, nesting material, droppings, and other debris can collect beneath and around the solar array.

Bright Panels provides bird-proofing services designed to help restrict access beneath the panels. When appropriate, we clean accessible debris around the system and install protective mesh around the perimeter of the array.

Bird proofing is especially useful for properties experiencing repeated bird activity, nesting material, or debris beneath the panels.
